Paddleboarding Vancouver & British Columbia

Stand up paddleboarding in Vancouver and British Columbia

SUP19 Vancouver is a personal paddleboarding project. We share short, practical posts about paddling safely on the ocean and on BC lakes: safety rules, cold water, tides, wind and gear.

Safety first. Every fact in our posts comes from an official source, such as Transport Canada, Environment and Climate Change Canada, Fisheries and Oceans Canada, the Canadian Safe Boating Council or the Lifesaving Society. Each post names its sources.
