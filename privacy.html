This website

This website is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics, ads or forms, and we do not collect personal information on this site. GitHub, as the host, may process technical data such as IP addresses under its own privacy statement.

SUP19 Vancouver Publisher app

SUP19 Vancouver Publisher is a private tool that publishes our own posts to our own SUP19 Vancouver accounts on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. It is used only by the account owner and is not offered to other users.

  • What it accesses: only our own SUP19 Vancouver channel and accounts, to upload our posts and to read their publishing status and basic statistics.
  • Google user data: the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to our own channel and to read our own videos' status. It does not access any other user's data.
  • Storage: access tokens are stored only in the owner's local macOS Keychain. They are never shared, sold or sent to third parties, and are never stored in code or logs.
  • No sharing or selling: we do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or from Meta's business integrations settings.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API use is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Social media

When you visit our accounts on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube or TikTok, those platforms process your data under their own privacy policies. We only see what the platforms show to account owners, such as public comments and summary statistics.

Deletion requests

To ask about or delete any data related to you, email akincenk@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

Changes

We will update this page if anything changes and show the new date at the top.
